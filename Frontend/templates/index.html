How it works

  1. Upload

    Choose a kidney CT slice saved as a PNG or JPG file.

  2. Analyse

    A trained neural network examines the image and scores each possible class.

  3. Review

    You see the most likely class and how confident the model is about it.

What the model looks for

Normal

A healthy kidney with no visible abnormal structure.

Cyst

A round, fluid-filled sac. Most are harmless but some need follow-up.

Stone

A hard deposit of minerals and salts that can block the flow of urine.

Tumor

An abnormal growth of tissue that a doctor must examine further.
